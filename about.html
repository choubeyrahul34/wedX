About Us

WedX is the home fragrance brand of Ars Productions. We sell reed diffusers to customers in India, for rooms that should feel settled and for people who would rather give one considered object than a loud one.

The product on this website is the Oud Reed Diffuser, 250 ml. It is crafted with premium essential oils and releases a soft, lingering aroma that warms without overpowering. It is toxin-free and thoughtfully made. It belongs in a dining room, a study, a bedroom, or a bathroom, and it is meant to be given.

Ovia is our gift house. From that page a buyer can send the diffuser with a short note, for a new home, a thank-you, a wedding, or a quiet desk.

Prices are shown in Indian rupees and include applicable taxes. An order is placed on this website with a delivery address in India. Online payment is collected through our payment gateway by UPI, cards, or netbanking. Pay on delivery is offered where that choice is shown at checkout. We pack confirmed orders and ship them across India. The shipping page and the Refund/Cancellation Policy explain timing, charges, and what happens if you need to cancel or return.
